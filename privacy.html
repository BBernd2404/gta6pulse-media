Privacy Policy – GTA6 Pulse Stats

Last updated: 21 November 2026

GTA6 Pulse Stats only accesses the TikTok account of its owner (@gta6pulse2404), with the owner's consent via TikTok Login Kit.

Data accessed: basic profile information and public statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares, captions, publish dates).

Use: the data is stored only on the owner's computer to evaluate which posts perform well. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

No third-party data: the tool does not access other users' accounts or personal data.

Revocation: access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

Contact: via the GTA6 Pulse profile on Instagram or TikTok (@gta6pulse2404).
